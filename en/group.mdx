## Regulatory status

Rise is not currently licensed or supervised as a credit institution, payment institution, or investment firm. Payment and banking functionality in the Rise platform is provided through licensed partners, who are named in our [Subprocessors list](/en/privacy/subprocessors). We will update this page if that changes.

Accounting services are provided by KLT-qualified accountants and follow the Finnish accounting profession's standards of good practice.

For data protection matters, the competent supervisory authorities are listed in section 16 of our [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/privacy-policy).
