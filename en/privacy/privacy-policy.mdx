## 1. Introduction

Rise Financial Group AG ("**Rise**", "**we**", "**us**", or "**our**"), registered under CHE-276.208.367 and domiciled in Stans, Switzerland, is the parent company of the Rise group. The group currently includes:

- **Rise Financial Group AG** (Switzerland) — parent company, group governance and oversight

This Privacy Policy covers both:

- our marketing website at [risegroup.eu](https://risegroup.eu) (the "**Website**"), and
- the **Rise platform and accounting service** (the "**Service**")

We are committed to processing your personal data in compliance with:

- **EU General Data Protection Regulation** (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679)
- **Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection** (FADP / nDSG)
- **Finnish Data Protection Act** (Tietosuojalaki, 1050/2018)

## 2. Our two roles

This is the most important thing to understand about how we handle data, so we put it first.

<Info>
**We are the controller** for data about you as a visitor, a prospect, and a user of our Service — your account, your contact details, how you use the platform. This policy describes that processing.

**Rise Technologies Oy is the processor** for the accounting data you put into Rise — your receipts, invoices, payroll records, and bank transactions, including the personal data of your employees and business partners. There, **you** are the controller and your service agreement is with Rise Technologies Oy. That processing is governed by the Data Processing Agreement in that agreement, not by this policy. Rise Financial Group AG acts as a subprocessor for group administration.
</Info>

In plain terms: this policy explains what we do with data that is *about you*. What we do with the data you *entrust to us* to keep your books is set out in your contract, where you decide the purposes and we follow your instructions.

## 4. Personal data we collect

### 4.1 Website visitors

- Name, email address, company name, and phone number — if you submit a contact or signup form
- Communication preferences
- IP address, browser type and version, operating system
- Access timestamps, referring URLs, pages visited and interaction data (via analytics, with your consent)

### 4.2 Service users

When you or your colleagues use the Rise platform, we process:

- **Account data** — name, work email address, phone number, job role, and user permissions
- **Authentication data** — login timestamps, device and browser information, session identifiers
- **Usage data** — which features are used and when, in the form of events and technical metadata
- **Support communications** — messages you send us and our replies

### 4.3 Identity verification data

When you sign a service agreement, we verify the identity of your authorised representatives. Our verification provider collects and checks identity documents, a facial image for liveness confirmation, and screening results. **This data is held by the verification provider, not in Rise** — we store only the verification reference and its outcome.

### 4.4 Messages

Messages you exchange with your accountant inside the Rise application, including any attachments you send.

### 4.5 Business contact data

We hold contact details for people at customer and prospect companies — name, work email address, company, and role — in our customer relationship system. See section 6.

### 4.6 Data you upload to the Service

Receipts, invoices, payroll records, bank transactions and supporting documents you or your employees submit to Rise. **We process this as your processor, under your instructions.** It is covered by your Data Processing Agreement, not by this policy.

## 6. Business contact data and enrichment

We use **Attio** as our customer relationship system. It holds business contact details of people at customer and prospect companies: name, work email address, company, and role.

<Note>
**Enrichment.** Attio's enrichment features are enabled in our account. Business contact details may be sent to third-party data providers, some of them outside the EEA, which return additional publicly available business information about the company or role. We rely on our legitimate interest in maintaining accurate business records (Art. 6(1)(f)).

This concerns business contact data only. It never involves accounting data, receipts, payroll records, or bank transactions.

You can object to this processing at any time under Art. 21 — write to [support@risegroup.eu](mailto:support@risegroup.eu) and we will remove your record from enrichment.
</Note>

Attio's own subprocessor list, including the enrichment providers and their locations, is published at [trust.attio.com/subprocessors](https://trust.attio.com/subprocessors).

## 7. Artificial intelligence

The Rise Service uses AI to read receipts and invoices, propose bookkeeping entries, and flag anomalies.

- Inference runs on **Amazon Bedrock in Frankfurt**, using Anthropic's Claude model
- Your data stays in the EU and is not sent to the United States for processing
- Bedrock does not store prompts or model responses, and the model developer receives no access to your data
- **Your data is never used to train AI models**
- Bookkeeping entries that materially affect your accounts are reviewed by a KLT-qualified accountant before they are posted

You are not subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22).

### Data sources you connect

Rise can read accounting material directly from systems you already use — your previous accounting system, or your email and calendar at Google Workspace or Microsoft 365. You decide which sources to connect.

**These sources are not our subprocessors.** They remain your own providers under your own agreements with them. You authorise our access, we use only what that authorisation permits, and you can withdraw it at any time.

For email and calendar, the authorisation is limited by design: we search for specific accounting documents rather than reading the mailbox as a whole, we store only the documents that belong in your accounts, and the permissions to delete, modify, or send are excluded from the authorisation request itself. You can revoke access directly from your Google or Microsoft account.

Once a document has been retrieved into Rise, it becomes part of the accounting data we process for you, and the Data Processing Agreement applies to it.

## 9. International data transfers

We process and store personal data primarily within the **EU/EEA**. Where data moves outside it, appropriate safeguards apply:

- **Switzerland** — covered by an EU adequacy decision (Rise Financial Group AG headquarters)
- **United States** — transfers to Webflow, Cloudflare, and Google are protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and EU Standard Contractual Clauses under Art. 46(2)(c)
- **Support access to EU infrastructure** — where AWS support staff may access data from outside the EEA, the AWS Data Processing Addendum and EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply
- **Enrichment providers** — see section 6 and Attio's subprocessor list for the safeguards that apply

Accounting data you upload is retained under the statutory periods set out in your service agreement — generally six years for accounting records and ten years for financial statements.

After the retention period expires, personal data is securely deleted or anonymised.

## 11. Security

- **Encryption in transit and at rest** — TLS for all communications; stored data encrypted
- **Access controls** — access to personal data on a need-to-know basis, with multi-factor authentication for staff
- **Infrastructure** — hosted on AWS in Frankfurt with enterprise-grade security controls
- **Logging and monitoring** — access to customer data is logged and reviewed
- **Incident response** — documented procedures for breach detection, containment, and notification

If a personal data breach occurs, we notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours where required, and affected individuals without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights.
