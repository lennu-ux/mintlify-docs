## 2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. They help the website recognise your device and remember information about your visit, such as your preferences or login status.

Similar technologies include:

- **Local storage:** Data stored in your browser that persists across sessions
- **Session storage:** Data stored temporarily and cleared when you close the browser tab
- **Pixels and web beacons:** Small images embedded in web pages or emails used to track interactions

## 3. How we use cookies

We categorise our cookies into four groups based on their purpose.

### 3.1 Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the Website to function. They cannot be disabled.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Session cookie | Rise / Webflow | Maintains your session while browsing the site | Session |
| CSRF token | Rise / Webflow | Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks | Session |
| Cookie consent | Rise | Stores your cookie consent preferences | 12 months |
| `__cf_bm` | Cloudflare (via Webflow) | Bot detection and security — distinguishes human visitors from automated bots | 30 minutes |

### 3.2 Functional cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalisation.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |

### 3.3 Analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our Website so we can improve it. They collect information anonymously.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga`, `_ga*` | Google Analytics | Tracks page views, sessions, and user journeys to help us understand website usage | Up to 2 years |
| `_gid` | Google Analytics | Distinguishes unique visitors | 24 hours |

<Info>
We use **Google Analytics 4** with IP anonymisation on the Website. No personally identifiable information is sent to Google. Analytics cookies are only set if you give your consent.

We currently do **not** use marketing or advertising cookies. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and request your consent before setting any marketing cookies.

## 5. Cookie consent

When you first visit our Website, a **cookie consent banner** is displayed, allowing you to:

- **Accept all cookies** — enables all cookie categories
- **Reject non-essential cookies** — only strictly necessary cookies are set
- **Customise preferences** — choose which optional cookie categories to enable

You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the cookie settings link in the footer of our Website.

<Warning>
**Legal basis:** Non-essential cookies (functional, analytics) are set only with your **explicit consent** in accordance with the EU ePrivacy Directive and GDPR Article 6(1)(a). Strictly necessary cookies are set under GDPR Article 6(1)(f) — our legitimate interest in providing a secure and functional website.
</Warning>

## 6. How to manage cookies

### Via our cookie banner

Use the cookie consent banner on your first visit, or click the **"Cookie settings"** link in the Website footer at any time.

### Via your browser

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings:

- **Chrome:** `chrome://settings/cookies`
- **Firefox:** `about:preferences#privacy`
- **Safari:** Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
- **Edge:** `edge://settings/content/cookies`

<Note>
Blocking or deleting cookies may affect the functionality of our Website. Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled without impacting core functionality.
</Note>
