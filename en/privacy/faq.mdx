## Roles and responsibilities

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Who is the controller and who is the processor?">
It depends on which data you mean, and this is the distinction that matters most.

For **your accounting data** — receipts, invoices, payroll records, bank transactions — **you are the controller** and Rise Technologies Oy is the processor. You decide the purposes; we follow your instructions. This is governed by the Data Processing Agreement in your service agreement.

For data **about you** as a user — your account, contact details, how you use the platform — **Rise Financial Group AG is the controller**. That is governed by the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/privacy-policy).

Rise Financial Group AG also acts as a subprocessor for group administration.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which Rise company do we contract with?">
**Rise Technologies Oy** (Business ID 3512553-2, Jyväskylä, Finland) provides the accounting service and operates the platform. Your service agreement is with that company.

**Rise Financial Group AG** (CHE-276.208.367, Stans, Switzerland) is the parent company and publishes the group's legal documentation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do we need to sign a separate data processing agreement?">
No. The Data Processing Agreement is Annex 2 of the service agreement, so it is concluded when the service agreement is signed. It satisfies GDPR Article 28 and is in writing in electronic form as Article 28(9) requires.

If your own compliance process needs the DPA as a standalone document for your records, write to [support@risegroup.eu](mailto:support@risegroup.eu) and we will send it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a record of processing activities?">
Yes. Annex 2-A of the service agreement is the record of processing activities — the categories of data subjects, data categories, processing purposes, and retention periods. It is part of the signed package.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Where your data is

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Where is our data hosted?">
On **Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, Germany** (eu-central-1). The Rise platform, the database, and the documents you upload are all there.

The full picture of who processes what and where is on the [Subprocessors page](/en/privacy/subprocessors).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does any of our data leave the EU?">
Your accounting data is processed in the EU/EEA. Some processing touches countries outside it, and each case has a safeguard:

- **Switzerland** — Rise Financial Group AG, covered by an EU adequacy decision.
- **AWS support access** — where AWS support staff may access data from outside the EEA, the AWS Data Processing Addendum and EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply.
- **Push notifications** — device tokens and notification content reach Apple (United States) under Standard Contractual Clauses.
- **Our own business tools** — some are based in the United States. None of them receive your accounting data.

Section 9 of the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/privacy-policy) describes the transfer safeguards, and the [Subprocessors page](/en/privacy/subprocessors) names every provider and its processing location.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who inside Rise can see our accounting data?">
Access is granted on a need-to-know basis and staff accounts require multi-factor authentication. Access to customer data is logged and reviewed. Your accountant sees your data because the work requires it.

Section 11 of the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/privacy-policy) describes the security measures, and Annex 2-B of the service agreement sets them out in detail.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Artificial intelligence

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Is our data used to train AI models?">
No. Your data is never used to train AI models.

Inference runs on **Amazon Bedrock in Frankfurt** using Anthropic's Claude model. Bedrock does not store prompts or model responses, and the model developer receives no access to your data — Anthropic is the model supplier, not a processor of your information.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does AI make decisions about our accounts on its own?">
No. Bookkeeping entries that materially affect your accounts are reviewed by a KLT-qualified accountant before they are posted.

You are not subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects, within the meaning of GDPR Article 22.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Subprocessors

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How will we hear about a new subprocessor?">
We notify you **at least 30 days before** the change takes effect, to the data protection contact address you gave in the service agreement. You do not need to ask to be on the list.

You may object within 30 days. If we cannot resolve the concern, either party may terminate the affected part of the service, as set out in the Data Processing Agreement.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if a subprocessor has to be replaced urgently?">
If a provider ceases operations or has to be replaced at short notice, we notify you without delay. Your right to object is unaffected.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are the systems we connect ourselves subprocessors?">
No. Your previous accounting system, and your Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 email and calendar, remain **your own providers under your own agreements**. You authorise our access, we use only what that authorisation permits, and you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the rest of the service.

Once a document has been retrieved into Rise, it becomes accounting data we process for you, and the Data Processing Agreement applies to it from that point.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Email and calendar access

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What exactly do you read in our employees' mailboxes?">
We search for specific accounting documents — receipts, invoices, booking confirmations — rather than reading the mailbox as a whole. We store only the documents that belong in your accounts. Everything else stays where it is.

The permissions to **delete, modify, or send are excluded from the authorisation request itself**, not merely from our internal rules. An employee can revoke access at any time directly from their Google or Microsoft account.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can an employee refuse to grant access?">
Yes. Refusing, or withdrawing access later, has no negative consequences for the employee, and no reason has to be given. Receipts and expense claims can be submitted the normal way instead, for example by uploading them to the Rise platform.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Retention, exit and incidents

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What happens to our data if we stop using Rise?">
Accounting data is retained for the statutory periods set out in your service agreement — generally six years for accounting records and ten years for financial statements.

Account data, in-app messages and similar records are kept for the duration of the service agreement and then 12 months. Section 10 of the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/privacy-policy) has the full retention table.

After the retention period expires, personal data is securely deleted or anonymised.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we export our data?">
Yes. Rise provides an API and MCP access to your own data, and this is included in the monthly fee on every service level. The right to data portability under GDPR Article 20 applies in addition.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How fast do you report a data breach?">
We notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours where required, and affected individuals without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights.

Where Rise acts as your processor, we notify you without undue delay so that you can meet your own obligations as controller.
</Accordion>
