## What will be published here

The Rise service agreement is a standard agreement: the same terms apply to every customer, and only the order form differs. When the migration is finished, this section will contain:

| | Document | Language |
|---|---|---|
| | **Service agreement terms** — parties, the standard service, evolving service, transition service, order of precedence, term, liability, confidentiality | Finnish, English |
| Annex 1 | **Price annex** — service levels, monthly fees, transaction prices, volume discount, hourly services | Finnish, English |
| Annex 2 | **Data processing agreement** — GDPR Article 28 terms, with the record of processing activities (2-A) and the security measures (2-B) | Finnish, English |
| Annex 3 | **Service description** — what the standard service contains | Finnish, English |
| Annex 4 | **SaaS terms** — the Rise platform | Finnish, English |
| Annex 5 | **Code of conduct** | Finnish, English |
| Annex 6 | **TAL2023 general terms** — referenced, published by the Finnish Association of Accounting Firms | Finnish |
| Annex 7 | **Power of attorney** — transition from a previous provider | Finnish, English |

The annex numbering matches the numbering used inside the agreement and in every signed copy. It will not change.

## The Swiss edition

A separate edition of the service agreement exists for customers contracting under Swiss law. It follows the same structure and annex numbering. It is currently in draft and is not yet published.
