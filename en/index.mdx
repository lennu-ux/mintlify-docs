Rise Financial Group AG is a financial technology group building AI-powered services for European SMEs. We believe in transparency, simplicity, and earning your trust — starting with how we handle your data and define our relationship with you.

The documents here apply across the Rise group: Rise Financial Group AG and every company under it, including Rise Technologies Oy, which develops and operates the Rise platform. Where a document concerns one company specifically, it says so.
