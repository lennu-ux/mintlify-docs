Each document on this site carries a version number and an effective date. When a version is replaced, the old one stays available at its own address, so you can always see the text that applied when you signed.

## How versions work

<Steps>

<Step title="Your agreement names a version">
Your service agreement refers to the standard terms at a specific version — for example `legal.risegroup.eu/en/terms/v1.2`. That version is the one that applies to you, and it does not change underneath you.
</Step>

<Step title="We notify you before a new version takes effect">
Changes to the price annex and to service level feature descriptions require **three months' written notice**. Changes to the subprocessor list require **30 days**. The notice goes to the contact address in your service agreement.
</Step>

<Step title="You can object or terminate">
If you do not accept a change, you may terminate under the termination terms of your service agreement. The notice always says what changed and by when you have to act.
</Step>

<Step title="Old versions stay published">
Superseded versions remain reachable at their own addresses indefinitely. We do not edit a version after it has been superseded.
</Step>

</Steps>

Versions earlier than 1.1 were not published on this site. If you signed before 11 September 2026, the text that applies to you is the PDF attached to your signed agreement. Write to [support@risegroup.eu](mailto:support@risegroup.eu) if you need another copy.
</Note>
